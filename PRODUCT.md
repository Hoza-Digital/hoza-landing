# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Businesses in Indonesia, Singapore, and worldwide that need a capable partner to design and build websites, applications, mobile products, automation, or custom software. Visitors are evaluating credibility, delivery speed, and fit before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Hoza as a focused digital product studio, explain its capabilities and working process clearly, demonstrate credible outcomes, and turn qualified interest into project enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Fast, confident, and technical. The voice is direct, business-focused, and energetic without becoming loud or vague.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS-template styling, timid visual systems, empty technology claims, unnecessary agency jargon, and decorative complexity that slows comprehension.

## Design Principles

- Make forward momentum tangible.
- Show business outcomes, not just technical capability.
- Keep the path from interest to enquiry direct.
- Use motion to reinforce speed and precision.
- Earn confidence through clarity and specificity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic structure, sufficient color contrast, responsive behavior, and reduced-motion alternatives.
